Work / Breezy

Breezy – A breath-controlled game for children

A flute, four animal friends and a calmer child. Breezy makes breathing practice feel like play, designed to ease anxiety before medical tests and help children aged 5–10 keep practising.

My role
UX research, Product strategy, Interaction & System Design, Branding & Packaging
Scope
UI, UX, Interaction Design
Industry
Healthcare
Project type
Academic
Duration
3 weeks, 2026

Summary

  • What it is Breezy reimagines pediatric respiratory therapy by transforming clinical breathing exercises into an engaging digital-physical gaming experience. It uses mechanics like short puffs to hop across lily pads, breaks the monotonous loop and turns a daily medical chore into a fun skill-building experience.
  • At a glance 4 games, for children aged 5–10, with a flute as the game controller.
