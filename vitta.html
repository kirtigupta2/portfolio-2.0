Vitta – An AI banking assistant

From 12 fields to retype to 6 pre-filled fields to review. Vitta shows customers how a credit card’s benefits fit their spending & saves their progress, so they can return to your application right where you left off.

Role
UX Analyst Intern
Industry
FinTech / Banking
Duration
2 weeks, 2026
Platform
Mobile banking app
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Figma Make, GitHub

Summary

  • The brief Explore how agentic AI and an adaptive interface could simplify credit card discovery and application within a banking app, demonstrating a concept for potential banking clients.
  • My contribution In a four-member team, I mapped competitor journeys and friction points, proposed the agentic architecture, and translated the journey and information architecture into an adaptive UI and interactive prototype.
  • A note Created during my internship. The work shown is an independently recreated concept and differs from the confidential deliverables produced during the engagement.
